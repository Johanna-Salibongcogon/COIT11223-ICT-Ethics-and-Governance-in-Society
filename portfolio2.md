### Summary of the artefact
This is an educational video from YouTube explaining the ethical implications of AI in business, including its consequences, effects, and questions. It covers key ethical considerations in AI, such as accountability, transparency, privacy, the replacement of workers, and environmental effects. From what I have understood of this artefact is that there are benefits to the business because of Artificial Intelligence, however, it causes disruption and development and has ethical issues that can harm individuals and society.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact.
I’ve chosen this artefact, a YouTube video titled “Business School 101” to better comprehend the creation of Artificial Intelligence, which not only involves the creation of a highly intelligent system, but also involves ethical responsibility in creating this type of system. The main concept I picked up from this video is how AI can also learn biases from the data that it is trained with, and this can result in biased consequences that can impact a person's life. I was impressed before I saw this artefact, as I thought how amazing it would be to have AI tools that do most of the work and enhance productivity in most of the tasks. After watching the artefact, I learned that there are a lot of ethical issues, such as Amazon's AI recruitment tool that have a discrimination against women when hiring, and it can also affect environmental issues. Artificial Intelligence is now transforming ICT as it can be incorporated into information systems like automation, analysis of data, and supporting decision-making. Learning about the key idea of Artificial Intelligence, I feel both good and bad about it, because Artificial Intelligence can help me as a person and can solve complex problems. But I believe some problems need limits, and there should be more space for humans to take control of Artificial Intelligence, and that it should not do too much, which will affect every individual, society, and mostly our environment.



## Artefact 2: Rise of AI influencers raising ethical concerns 
<img width="1854" height="826" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e5c857be-4f2a-4878-91e6-cf15f78cda31" />
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5kjxBd16sm4

### Summary of the artefact
This artefact is a news video on YouTube by the British Broadcasting Channel (BBC) news delivering about the rise of Artificial Intelligence influencers in modelling, on how they are becoming popular, and how it affects the perception of model standards. The video explains how a model named Atina that was made by Artificial Intelligence earns from sponsorships and promotes products. From what I have understood from the artefact, Artificial Intelligence can now create a beautiful influencer that can earn through promotions and sponsorships, but it also raises ethical issues about the perception of beauty standards that are unrealistic and how it influences society, especially young people.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact.
I chose this artefact because it provides different use cases regarding Artificial Intelligence. The key idea that I have learned is that you can now create a model using Artificial Intelligence and can earn through promotions and sponsorships. Before watching this artefact, I thought that it was better to advertise to real people, especially to famous and beautiful people, but after watching this artefact, you can advertise using a virtual influencer that behaves human and has unrealistic beauty to promote and sponsor your product. You can now convince some companies to promote their product using your virtual influencer. This type of Artificial Intelligence is part of ICT because it uses social media platforms, digital marketing, and creates content to connect and convince people. With this type of Artificial Intelligence, it can affect society, especially among teenagers who are women who compare themselves to unrealistic appearances. This made me realize that social media promotes unrealistic attributes such as appearances even before Artificial Intelligence, but now you can earn through promotions and sponsorships with this kind of content. I have negative feelings about this kind of technology because it promotes unrealistic beauty standards and how it manipulates the perception of the real beauty of every human being.



## Artefact 3: Navigating ethical considerations in software development and deployment in technological giants
<img width="1915" height="1000" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8906bce1-9ca5-4c7d-8e6b-09513fc33483" />
https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Daniel-Ajiga/publication/383409906_Navigating_ethical_considerations_in_software_development_and_deployment_in_technological_giants/links/66cb6190c2eaa5002318676f/Navigating-ethical-considerations-in-software-development-and-deployment-in-technological-giants.pdf

### Summary of the artefact
This artefact is an ethics journal article in the International Journal of Engineering Research Updates about ethics in large technology companies in the deployment and development of software applications. This artefact discusses some of the ethical challenges of data privacy, machine learning algorithm bias, transparency, accountability, and the implications of software and AI in a society that is constantly and rapidly evolving. (Ajiga et al. 2024, pp. 51–54). It involves a keen emphasis on user privacy, safe data usage, adherence to regulations like GDPR and CCPA, and reducing bias to make sure that algorithms are fair. Many lessons were learnt from this artefact not only about the new technology being developed, but also about the ethics, responsibility, and benefits of developing technologies for society.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact.
I chose this artefact because it provides the ethical duties of software developers that relate to Information and Communication Technology (ICT). In Information Technology, we learnt about user privacy, how to minimize bias in AI algorithms, how to develop transparency and accountability of algorithms, and what the responsibilities of a developer are. Before reading this artefact, I didn't think about any ethical concerns of technology other than privacy and cybersecurity concerns with data (Ajiga et al. 2024, pp. 51–54). After reading this artefact, I realize the kind of impact that software applications have and the wider social impacts if they are not used ethically. These ethical guidelines can benefit society because people use software systems in education, business, finance, and many other areas that are used in society. In my experience, I have a duty to adhere to these ethical guidelines in development and deployment because I have a responsibility to ensure that users' data is secure, reduce the risk of bias, security breaches, and the misuse of Artificial Intelligence. I feel positive about this artefact because it makes me thrive more, not only to develop and deploy applications, but I have a duty to protect and secure the data of the users.
