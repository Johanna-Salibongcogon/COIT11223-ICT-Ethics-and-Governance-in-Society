## Artefact 1: What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

### Summary of the artefact

This is a webpage of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration that defines what Artificial Intelligence is. It discusses the ability of a computer system to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, like reasoning, learning, decision-making, and problem-solving. It talks about how Machine Learning is a subset of AI, it uses Machine Learning for computers to learn from data. From what I have understood, Artificial Intelligence is a technology that aims to mimic human intelligence by requiring reasoning, learning, and decision-making. This artefact is meaningful evidence of society because it will help society understand the fundamental concept of what Artificial Intelligence is.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact.

I chose this artefact because NASA is an organization that is a highly incredible source in technology, and NASA has explained clearly what artificial intelligence really is a system that can do complicated tasks that are done by humans, like decision-making, reasoning, etc. NASA also showed how it works in the real world, Artificial Intelligence can perform tasks on its own when a situation or data changes. NASA also explained that Artificial Intelligence achieved this by using data and algorithms to teach computers to make classifications, and it can generate predictions to solve complex problems or to handle tasks. NASA also provided different types of concepts that are used in Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Decision Support, Natural Language Processing, and Neural Networks.

### Reflection

What I have learned from this artefact is that Artificial Intelligence (AI) is not just a chatbot or a robot. Artificial Intelligence is a technology that aims to mimic human intelligence by requiring reasoning, learning, and decision-making. This artefact demonstrates my learning because of how clear its explanation of the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence is. It links to the unit material because it introduces the core concepts of Artificial Intelligence and its different concepts. This artefact is meaningful evidence of society because it will help society understand the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence.

## Artefact 3: I have found the history of Artificial Intelligence
<img width="850" height="892" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6aeba12a-730d-4f74-9356-3fdc7c395f56" />
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0738081X23002687

### Summary of the artefact
This is a scholarly journal article that explains the history of artificial intelligence. It was written by Andrzej Grzybowski, Krzysztof Pawlikowski, and Claude Lambert in 2024. In 1804, the Jacquard Loom introduced the idea of storing punched cards for instructions Grzybowski, Pawlikowska–Łagód & Lambert, 2024, pp. 2–3. In a later part, it was Charles Babbage who first designed the concept of a mechanical computer called "Difference Engine". From what I have understood, the concept of the Jacquard Loom was the birth of Artificial Intelligence by storing instructions on punched cards.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact.
I chose this article because it is important to know the history of Artificial Intelligence and how it started. In this article, I now know that simply storing instructions on punched cards can ignite an amazing technology. This article also showed how Artificial Intelligence developed over time, and the important contributions by different remarkable people such as Alan Turing, who famously deciphered the code of the Enigma machine in World War 2, Charles Babbage, who conceptualized the Analytical Engine, and also Ada Lovelace, who was the first person to create the first computer program. Ada Lovelace foresaw that a machine could not only manipulate numbers but also symbols such as alphabetical letters. Learning the origins of Artificial Intelligence provides a better understanding of today's technologies.
