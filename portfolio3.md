## Artefact 1: Intellectual Property
<img width="1918" height="883" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/10959c6c-0304-4379-823d-13e7acc6772a" />
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oj_VwmI-Q18

### Summary of the artefact

This educational video from youtube explains the various types of Intellectual Property such as Trade Secrets, Trademarks, copyright and patents. The video provides a basic overview of the concept of trade secrets, detailing that they are confidential business information that provides the company with a commercial advantage, but must be kept confidential. Artistic works (books, music and films, drawings, etc.) are protected by copyright while non-obvious, useful inventions are protected by patents. As far as I understand, Intellectual Property is a way for people and organisations to protect their ideas and creations, their brands, confidential information, and their inventions. It's a technology artefact, since software and digital productions can also relate to Intellectual Property.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact

I choose this youtube artefact because it demonstrates a knowledge and understanding of Intellectual Property as well as the different ways in which creations can be protected. I have come to know in the video that there are different types of Intellectual Property and each has its own purpose.. The aspect of trade secrets was intriguing to me, as I could see that having confidential information may provide the company with a commercial edge, unless the company is diligent in keeping it confidential. The examples of Nike and Apple also made me understand the importance of trademarks to companies as it helps people to distinguish their products. I was also taught about copyright – how the expression of an idea is protected, not the idea itself. I found the patent section to be very useful for gaining insight into how an invention can be protected where it is new, non-obvious and useful. This artefact relates to the unit because as an IT student, I could produce some software, designs, code or digital work that might include Intellectual Property. It was a reminder that there can be ethical and legal issues when using someone else's work without their permission.  

## Artefact 2: The 10 benefits of Intellectual Property

### Summary of the artefact

This article helps me understand what is Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) and its benefits for individuals and to the organisations. It explains that IPR gives legal protection to creative and innovative works like art, music, literature, technology, designs, trademarks, etc. This article explores various forms of IPR, such as copyright, patents, trademarks and geographical indications. It also addresses with students the advantages of having IPR, including legal protection, economic benefits, competitiveness, invention and creativity, reputation of business and economic independence. From what I have heard, IP rights can allow a creator or business to stop such utilization without consent of the creator, and can offer chances for the creator or business to generate some income from licensing and new business ventures. The article also highlights that IPR may be a tool that can motivate individuals to further develop and innovate.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact

I chose this artefact because it is a clear and simple explanation of Intellectual Property Rights and why they are important to the creator, inventor and business. I have learnt from the article that IPR is not just about protecting someone's work but also it may be of economic benefits as well as business opportunities. It said that creators can earn royalties by licensing and protected intellectual property can boost the value of a business. The section on innovation and creativity was also significant for me as this idea of protecting research and development will encourage people and organisations to develop new ideas without fear of anyone copying them. This artefact is relevant to the unit because as an IT student I could produce a software, a design, a website or some other electronic creation that may fall under the umbrella of Intellectual Property.

## Artefact 3: Artificial Intelligence and Intellectual Property: An Economic Perspective
<img width="1918" height="951" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/90f73b74-b0e0-45e5-bc04-d4377482c0b8" />
https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4757971

### Summary of the artefact

This is a scholarly artefact that explains the relationship between Artificial Intelligence and Intellectual Property. It is a work by Alexander Cuntz, Carsten Fink and Hansueli Stamm. The article examines the effects of AI on innovation and creativity, and the future of IP laws for AI-based inventions and AI-based works. It also expresses that AI tools can stimulate and spark human creativity, and yet it can also create works with minimal human effort (Cuntz, Fink & Stamm, 2024, pp. 1–3). What I have read is that Artificial Intelligence is influencing the way people make and innovate and which is creating new challenges to Intellectual Property.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact

This artefact allows me to understanding how useful AI is in the development of various forms of innovations, including AI-assisted and AI-generated innovations (Cuntz, Fink & Stamm, 2024, pp. 6–8). This gave me an idea that there are different levels of human involvement in Artificial Intelligence using AI as a tool vs creating something with minimal human involvement. This artefact is relevant to the unit because as an IT student I can use Artificial Intelligence to produce software, content or other digital creations. When I learned about Intellectual Property Rights and the use of AI, it made me aware of the company who about their legal and ethical issues regarding ownership and the rights of the content that was generated by AI.
