## Artefact 1: Intellectual Property
<img width="1918" height="883" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/10959c6c-0304-4379-823d-13e7acc6772a" />
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oj_VwmI-Q18

### Summary of the artefact

This educational video from youtube that explains the various types of Intellectual Property: Trade Secrets, Trademarks, copyright and Patents. The video provides a basic overview of the concept of trade secrets, detailing that they are confidential business information that provides the company with a commercial advantage, but must be kept confidential. Artistic works (books, music and films, drawings, etc.) are protected by copyright while non-obvious, useful inventions are protected by patents. As far as I understand, Intellectual Property is a way for people and organisations to safeguard their ideas and creations, their brands, confidential information, and their inventions. It's a technology artefact, since software and digital productions can also relate to Intellectual Property.

### Justification on why I chose this artefact

I chose this youtube video artefact as it shows an understanding and explanation of Intellectual Property and how different creations can be protected. Through the video, I have learned that there are various kinds of Intellectual Property and each has a different purpose. The aspect of trade secrets was intriguing to me, as I could see that having confidential information may provide the company with a commercial edge, unless the company is diligent in keeping it confidential. The examples of Nike and Apple also made me understand the importance of trademarks to companies as it helps people to distinguish their products. I was also taught about copyright – how the expression of an idea is protected, not the idea itself. I found the patent section to be very useful for gaining insight into how an invention can be protected where it is new, non-obvious and useful. This artefact relates to the unit because as an IT student, I could produce some software, designs, code or digital work that might include Intellectual Property. It was a reminder that there can be ethical and legal issues when using someone else's work without their permission. 

## Artefact 2: The 10 benefits of Intellectual Property
<img width="1918" height="952" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4aa96ccb-8b3a-4d12-93c5-15b37d8529e9" />
https://widjojo.id/benefits-of-intellectual-property-rights/

### Summary of the artefact

This is an educational article to understand what Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) and what are its benefits for individuals and organisations. It explains that IPR gives legal protection to creative and innovative outputs like art, music, literature, technology, designs, trademarks, etc. The article explores various forms of IPR, such as copyright, patents, trademarks and geographical indications. It also addresses with students the advantages of having IPR, including legal protection, economic benefits, competitiveness, invention and creativity, reputation of business and economic independence. From what I have heard, IP rights can allow a creator or business to stop such utilization without consent of the creator, and can offer chances for the creator or business to generate some income from licensing and new business ventures. The article also highlights that IPR may be a tool that can motivate individuals to further develop and innovate.
